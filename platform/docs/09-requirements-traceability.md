# Requirements traceability

This document re-checks every instruction the owner has given against what actually exists. It was first written on 2026-09-28, when the owner asked for "a recheck of every prompt and make sure your current direction is focused". It was last brought up to date on 2026-10-01, when the owner made Wisconsin Surplus the first priority, for a resale business run with this tool.

**Status legend**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DONE** | Built and verified against real data or a real system. |
| **PARTIAL** | Some of it exists; the rest is named explicitly. |
| **IN PROGRESS** | Being built now. |
| **LATER** | Sequenced behind something it depends on. |
| **BLOCKED** | Needs the owner, or an outside party, to act. |

"Verified" always means run against production or a live site, never against a fixture written from documentation. Documentation-based testing is how the GSA adapter once passed 24 tests while returning zero real lots.

## Direction check

Coverage is the product. The work in flight, in order:

1. **Wisconsin Surplus first (row 37).** The owner sends the letter in `docs/10` section 1. The sale cards switch on the day permission arrives, and single items follow if the reply allows them.
2. **Permission for the other held Wisconsin sources (rows 19, 33).** The adapters are built and verified; what is missing is each operator's written permission (drafts in `docs/10`), or an owner decision on their terms.
3. **Permitted sources next.** IRS Auctions is live, and so are the five BidWrangler houses, Dane County's tax-deed sale and the Department of Revenue's sale list (row 38). Treasury follows once the probe shows it reachable from Supabase. BidSpotter, Purple Wave and ShopGoodwill, listed here before, turned out to forbid automated collection in their terms and are held (0022), as are Farmers Hot Line, Farm Auction Guide and AuctionGuy (0037).
4. **A public deployment of the app.** Before publishing, sources that restrict redisplaying content (PropertyRoom, Hansen) should link out rather than show their photos.
5. **Blocked on the owner:**
   - adding https://www.waystock.org to Supabase Auth's redirect URLs, so magic-link sign-in returns to the live site (password sign-in works without it);
   - the HiBid terms decision;
   - an email provider key (row 4);
   - the eBay keyset (row 9);
   - an image embedding or vision key (row 17);
   - a GSA API key, to replace the shared demo key;
   - a Skeuos domain for the crawler's identity (row 11).

Nothing in flight is off-requirement. The lawful answer to sites that refuse our crawler, by bot protection or by their terms, stays the same: deep links, licensed data, partnerships. Never bypassing bot protection.
